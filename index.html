Warrior Tank Guide: Tauren Horde

Protection Warrior for World of Warcraft: Forever, from level 1 to 60. Sources checked on November 17, 2026. Devastate does not appear in any source. Shield Slam (level 40 talent) is the main Protection ability.

Most sources were written before launch or during the beta. Warcraft Tavern's Protection and leveling guides are beta-only and capped at level 30. Items marked Sources differ list each source's version. Items marked Not confirmed were not found in the sources I could read.

2. Rotation and tanking, levels 30 to 60

  • Single target (Icy Veins): Battle Shout, Sunder Armor and Demoralizing Shout kept up, then Shield Block, Shield Slam, Revenge, Thunder Clap, Rend, Sunder Armor, Heroic Strike. Sources differ on whether Shield Slam or Revenge comes first. Icy Veins puts Shield Slam first. Mobalytics lists Revenge first.
  • AoE (Icy Veins): Thunder Clap (up to 4 targets), Shield Slam, Revenge, Sunder Armor, Cleave.
  • Shield Block: 10 rage, +75% block chance for 7 seconds or 2 blocks, 5 second cooldown, off the global cooldown. Confirmed by Wowhead, Icy Veins and Mobalytics.
  • Shield Slam: 20 rage, 6 second cooldown, scales with Block Value, 50% chance to dispel a magic effect. Rage cost from Icy Veins only (Not confirmed elsewhere).
  • Revenge: 5 rage, 5 second cooldown (Icy Veins only).
  • Thunder Clap: 6 second cooldown, usable in Defensive Stance. Confirmed by all three sources.
  • Heroic Strike and Cleave: rage dump. Sources differ on the threshold: Wowhead suggests 30+ rage, Mobalytics suggests 50 or 60+.

3. Weapons, shield and the level 30 weapon quest

  • One-hander and shield to 60: Mobalytics (KallTorak) and Icy Veins. Pick the highest DPS one-hander and the highest armor shield. After level 40, Block Value also matters because it powers Shield Slam. Confirmed
  • Horde weapon rewards: Hammer of Orgrimmar (Ragefire Chasm), Wingblade (Wailing Caverns), Outlaw Sabre (Blackfathom Deeps), Bludgeon of Betrayed Virtues (level 35, Desolace reputation), Thrash Blade (Maraudon). Icy Veins mentions Thun'grim's Axe from a Barrens quest. Mobalytics source only. Not confirmed that these are the best picks for tanking.
  • Level 30 Whirlwind Weapon quest: Tauren start it with Torm Ragetotem in Thunder Bluff. It goes through Fray Island and Alterac Mountains, with an elite level 40 boss (Polestar). Rewards: Whirlwind Axe, Sword or Warhammer. Confirmed that all three are two-handers with item level 40, +16 Strength and +15 Stamina (ConquestCapped, LFCarry). They are DPS weapons, not tank weapons.
  • Sources differ on where Berserker Stance comes from. Warcraft Tavern says "The Affray". Polestar lists it as the end of the Whirlwind chain.

8. Level 60 endgame build

  • Icy Veins Shield Slam tank: Arms 5, Fury 15, Protection 31. Named talents: Deflection, Anticipation, Shield Specialization, Master of Defense, Improved Shield Wall, Shield Slam, Cruelty, Enrage, Lingering Rage. Swaps for low rage are listed in the source.
  • Wowhead, Method, Warcraft Tavern: sources give different raid builds (4/15/32, 17/3/31, 3/16/32 in Mobalytics). Sources differ on the split.
  • Mobalytics' level 60 tab is marked "WIP". Raids open December 9, 2026.
  • Not confirmed as a single build. Only Icy Veins gives a full version, and it was written before launch.
