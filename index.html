Warrior Tank Guide: Tauren Horde

Protection Warrior leveling for World of Warcraft: Forever, from level 1 to 60. Launch data from November 2026. Several sources were written during beta, so check the marked items in game.

Most guides for this class were written during the WoW Forever beta, which was capped at level 30. Kami-Labs published a full 10 to 60 build on November 12, 2026. Warcraft Tavern says its builds were designed for the beta. Items marked Sources differ have conflicting info. Items marked Not confirmed were not found in the sources I could open.

Character summary

  • Race: Tauren, Horde. Class: Warrior. Spec for leveling and tanking: Protection.
  • Mobalytics calls Protection the class's most complete tank toolkit in Forever. Icy Veins calls it the fastest Warrior spec to level and recommends it.
  • Tauren racials: War Stomp, Plainsrunning, Endurance and Cultivation. Nature Resistance was removed. Not confirmed the exact effect of Endurance and Cultivation in Forever.
  • Tauren stamina and Endurance make Tauren strong for survivability, according to community guides. These sources are from older expansions, so treat them as context.

Weapons and shield

  • One-hander and shield all the way to 60: reported by Mobalytics (KallTorak), with no gold spent on respecs. Confirmed by one source.
  • Whirlwind Weapon (Axe, Sword or Warhammer) comes from a class quest at level 30 (Warcraft Tavern).
  • Heavier, slower weapons are recommended because they boost instant attacks like Whirlwind and Mortal Strike (Warcraft Tavern).
  • Gear stat priority from Warcraft Tavern: Weapon DPS, then Strength/Attack Power, Stamina, Agility, Armor. Polearms can be learned from level 20.
  • Specific shield and weapon names for tanking: Not confirmed.

Rotation and tanking

  • Core rotation for early levels, per Warcraft Tavern: Battle Shout, Charge, Rend and Heroic Strike, with Hamstring and Thunder Clap as options.
  • Protection rotation for the 30 to 60 range: Revenge, Rend, Thunder Clap and Heroic Strike, per Warcraft Tavern's beta build. Shield Slam and Shield Block timings: Not confirmed.
  • Defiance and Last Stand are in the leveling build for threat and survival. Use them as defensive cooldowns on pulls with elites.
